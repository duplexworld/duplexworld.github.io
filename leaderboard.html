What was run

Every enterprise world instantiates the same nine interaction shapes, three scenarios each. Pathfinding shares one of them and adds two that only navigation elicits. Hover a cell for what it is worth in conversations.

Plus twelve authored Pathfinding variants that are released but not run: 156 scenarios authored, 144 campaigned.

Table 3 for the per-world blocks and the pooled block, Table 14 for the per-type figures, which the paper publishes as point estimates with no interval, and the duration densities are computed by gen_violins.py from the 3,825 conversation records rather than transcribed.
